# LearnAIQ — Class 1 to Class 5 Expansion Walkthrough

The **LearnAIQ** platform has been expanded to support **Class 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5** for both Maths and Science subjects with full English and Telugu localization and incremental pricing ($10/mo to $30/mo).

---

## 🌟 Class 1 to Class 5 Expansion Summary

1. **Class 1**: **$10 / month** after 7-Day Free Trial. (*Basics: Counting, Body Parts, Plants*)
2. **Class 2**: **$15 / month** after 7-Day Free Trial (+$5 increment). (*Place Value, Living Things*)
3. **Class 3**: **$20 / month** after 7-Day Free Trial (+$5 increment). (*Fractions, States of Matter*)
4. **Class 4**: **$25 / month** after 7-Day Free Trial (+$5 increment). (*Large Numbers, Decimals, Solar System*)
5. **Class 5**: **$30 / month** after 7-Day Free Trial (+$5 increment). (*Angles, Organ Systems, Ecosystems*)
